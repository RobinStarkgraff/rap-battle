## Mic Drop League

**Sign the talent. Set the lineup. Drop the mic.**

You don't rap. You're the label boss. Sign MCs and their crew at sealed-bid auctions, put
them on stage in the right order, and watch them battle a friend's crew in a fully automatic
rap battle. Super Auto Pets meets a football manager, in a 90s block party.

**Play in your browser:** https://robinstarkgraff.github.io/rap-battle/ (desktop, mouse)

### How it plays

- **Sign talent.** The whole league shares one market of unique units. Everyone bids at the
  same time, in secret, over up to three bidding rounds, and the best bid wins. Scout for
  hidden prospects that only you can see.
- **Set the lineup.** 3 MCs on stage (Opener, Middle, Closer), 2 support members (DJ, Hype
  Man, Producer, Vocal Coach, Manager) and a bench. Positions and triggers make the combos:
  a DJ who rewards every bar, a Hype Man who gets the next MC up when one chokes, a
  Freestyler who comes alive as the Closer.
- **Watch the battle.** The front MCs trade bars until one chokes and leaves the stage. The
  crowd's hype powers some abilities, the beat builds with it, and every choke swings it.
  Leagues can also play *crowd vote* battles: three verses, and the crowd picks the winner of
  each.
- **Keep your crew.** Units grow with every battle, earn records and a second ability, ask
  for raises, get older and retire into your Hall of Fame. Salaries eat your income, so every
  signing is a choice.
- **Climb the league.** One league for your friend group: a pyramid of divisions with
  promotion and relegation, seasons that run over many short sittings, and AI managers who
  fill empty slots and run your crew while you're away.

### Playing with friends

The game is peer to peer: no accounts, no game server.

1. One player clicks **Start a league**, founds a crew (name, colours, logo) and picks the
   league size. The rest of the league is bots.
2. They quit to the title screen, click **Host a sitting** and share the 4-letter room code.
3. Friends click **Join a sitting**, type the code and found their crews. Each one takes a
   bot's place in the league (or joins at the next season if no bot is left).
4. The host starts the round. Play as many rounds as you like; you can stop after any round,
   and the next sitting carries on from there. Any member can host the next one.

Everyone's browser keeps a copy of the league, so the newest copy wins when you meet again.
You can also play alone: **Start a league** and play against the bots.

### Controls

Mouse only. Click, drag units in the lineup, and type names and room codes. Mute and volume
are in the bottom-left corner.

### Made with

TypeScript, Phaser 3, PeerJS (WebRTC) and zod, built with Vite. **All code, art and sound
were generated by AI** (Claude Code): every figure is drawn from simple shapes at runtime, and
the beat and sound effects are synthesised with WebAudio. There are no image or audio files.

### Known issues

The full list is in the repo's `docs/known-issues.md`. The main ones:

- Desktop browsers only, and only tested in Chromium (Chrome, Edge).
- Your league lives in your browser's storage. Clearing site data deletes your copy and your
  claim on your crew, and there is no export.
- Some strict networks (corporate firewalls, some mobile networks) can block the peer-to-peer
  connection or the public signalling server.
